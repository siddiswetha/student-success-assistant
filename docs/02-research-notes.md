## Template interview questions — students

Use these as a starting point. Ask follow-up questions when an answer is unclear.

1. Where do you first hear about an assignment deadline? Where else might the same deadline appear?
2. Walk me through the last time you found out about a deadline. What did you do next?
3. Have you ever missed a deadline? What happened, and what would have helped you catch it earlier?
4. How do you currently keep track of deadlines — a notebook, phone calendar, memory, something else?
5. How do you know your attendance in each subject right now? How confident are you in that number?
6. Has anyone ever told you your attendance was too low? When did you find out, and was there still time to recover?
7. If an assistant told you "you are at risk in this subject", what would you want it to show you so you could trust it?
8. What would make you ignore a weekly reminder? How often is too often?
9. What information would you never want shared with an advisor without your permission?

## Template interview questions — advisor / tutor

1. When do you usually first learn that a student is at risk — from attendance, a missed deadline, or something else?
2. How is attendance calculated here? Is it the same in every subject? (Record the exact rule — do not assume.)
3. What is the attendance threshold, and what happens when a student falls below it?
4. What would a useful early warning contain? What would a useless or misleading warning contain?
5. How would you use a weekly at-risk list, and what would you need before acting on it?
6. What privacy limits should apply to student data in this system?
