# The story — Student Success Assistant
![Ethan — example student avatar](../assets/avatar-ethan.svg)

**This is an EXAMPLE fictional story.** It is not a real student, a real quote, or a real result. It shows, in plain language, the problem this project solves.

## Meet Ethan

Ethan is a first-year student. An assignment deadline is announced on a physical notice board, but Ethan does not see it. He also misses several classes while he is ill, and he does not realise his attendance has fallen.

By the time Ethan discovers the missed deadline, it is too late. By the time he checks his attendance, he is below the 75% requirement with no recovery path left. He had no warning while there was still time to act.

## Before the product

Deadlines are spread across lectures, email, the learning system, and notice boards. Attendance is a number Ethan never sees until it is too late. A wrong warning, or a missed warning, destroys trust, so Ethan stops believing the system altogether.

## After the product

Ethan gives the assistant the notices, emails, and posts he receives. The assistant extracts the assignment title, subject, and deadline, and Ethan confirms every extraction before it is saved.

Ethan can see his attendance percentage and exactly how many more classes he needs to attend, with the maths shown. Once a week, he gets one digest: what is due this week, which subjects are at risk, and one suggested action per risk. He acts early, while there is still time.

## What the AI PM learns from this

- Early, accurate warning is the whole product. A late warning is no warning.
- A wrong deadline is a critical failure, so a human must confirm every extraction.
- Attendance figures must show their maths and what data they are based on.
- Privacy matters: the student's data is theirs alone, and an advisor is involved only with the student's consent.
